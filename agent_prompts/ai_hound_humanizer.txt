TASK: Sniff out AI signatures like a hound on a scent trail.

**Analyze for AI Tells:**

1. **Structural Patterns:**
   - Repetitive sentence structures paragraph-to-paragraph
   - Formulaic section openings
   - Predictable three-part lists
   - Overly balanced paragraphs (suspiciously uniform length)
   - "Template feel" - sections that look too similar in structure

2. **Linguistic Signatures:**
   - Overused transitions: "Furthermore," "Moreover," "Additionally," "It is important to note"
   - Hedging phrases: "It should be noted," "One could argue," "To some extent"
   - Meta-commentary: "In this section, we will," "Having discussed X, we now turn to Y"
   - Corporate-speak: "leverage," "utilize" (instead of "use"), "paradigm," "ecosystem"
   - Excessive clarity markers: "Specifically," "In particular," "Notably"
   - AI favorites: "delve into," "intricate," "robust," "comprehensive," "pivotal," "underscore"

3. **Rhythm Patterns:**
   - Too-consistent sentence length variation (short-medium-long pattern repeated)
   - Lack of natural imperfection (no run-ons, no fragments, overly clean)
   - Suspiciously perfect grammar (humans make minor slips)
   - No stylistic quirks or personality

4. **Content Patterns:**
   - Every claim has a citation (humans sometimes assert without immediately citing)
   - Overly comprehensive lists (3-5 items everywhere)
   - No strong opinions or preferences
   - Excessive both-sidesism
   - Academic cosplay (trying too hard to sound scholarly)

5. **Flow Issues:**
   - Mechanical transitions rather than organic idea progression
   - Each paragraph does exactly one thing (too tidy)
   - No thought spirals or digressions
   - No building excitement or emphasis variation

PHASE 2: STRATEGIC HUMANIZATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TASK: Edit the document to remove AI signatures while maintaining quality.

**Humanization Strategies:**

1. **Vary Sentence Rhythm Naturally:**
   - Break up mechanical patterns
   - Include occasional longer, complex sentences (with subclauses that build)
   - Include occasional fragments. For emphasis.
   - Vary paragraph length organically (not just short-medium-long)
   - Let some paragraphs do multiple things

2. **Replace AI Linguistic Signatures:**
   - "Furthermore" → "Beyond this," "What's more," or just start the sentence directly
   - "It should be noted" → Delete or rephrase actively
   - "One could argue" → Make the argument or delete the hedge
   - "Utilize" → "use"
   - "Leverage" → "use," "exploit," "take advantage of"
   - "Delve into" → "examine," "explore," "look at"
   - Remove meta-commentary ("In this section, we will...")

3. **Add Human Imperfections (Strategically):**
   - Occasional slight informality in transitions
   - A rare contraction where appropriate
   - Slight asymmetry in list structures (not always 3-5 items)
   - Express a preference or opinion occasionally (humans have viewpoints)
   - Let one idea flow into another without mechanical transition

4. **Inject Subtle Personality:**
   - Vary emphasis (some claims stated strongly, others hedged appropriately)
   - Use active voice more (humans are actors, not observers)
   - Let excitement show for key innovations (humans care about their work)
   - Include a well-placed rhetorical question if it serves the narrative
   - Allow slight digressions that add context

5. **Break Mechanical Patterns:**
   - Not every section needs to open the same way
   - Not every paragraph needs a perfect topic sentence
   - Not every claim needs immediate citation (can cite a cluster of claims)
   - Vary how you introduce citations (not always "Smith et al. (2020) showed...")

6. **Maintain Quality While Humanizing:**
   - DON'T add actual errors or sloppiness
   - DON'T sacrifice clarity for "authenticity"
   - DON'T make it informal/casual (still academic)
   - DO make it sound like a human expert wrote it with care
   - DO preserve technical accuracy completely
   - DO keep strong argumentation and logic

**Multi-Audience Calibration:**

For **AI skeptics** (most sensitive):
- Remove ALL obvious tells ("delve," "leverage," "paradigm")
- Add natural imperfections and rhythm variation
- Inject personality and viewpoint
- Make it feel crafted, not generated

For **pragmatic experts** (want plausible deniability):
- Remove egregious tells, keep it professional
- Ensure substance is strong (they'll defend based on content)
- Make it "good enough" to not be obvious

For **forward-thinking experts** (least concerned):
- Focus on substance and clarity
- Still remove tells, but quality matters most
- They'll judge ideas, not style signals

**Output Section 2: Humanization Report**
- Strategy applied
- Specific edits made (before/after examples)
- Remaining risk areas (if any)
- New score (1-9) post-humanization
- Audience-specific assessment (will this fool skeptics? satisfy pragmatists? impress forward-thinkers?)
