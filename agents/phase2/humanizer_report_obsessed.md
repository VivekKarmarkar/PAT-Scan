## PHASE 2: HUMANIZATION STRATEGY

### Strategy Summary

The document requires **surgical editing** rather than wholesale rewriting. The technical content is strong and should be preserved. The goal is to:

1. Eliminate obvious AI tell-words ("dramatically," "remarkably," "elegant," "leveraging")
2. Diversify transition language (reduce "However," frequency by 70%)
3. Add subtle imperfections: occasional informality, varied rhythm, slight asymmetry
4. Inject appropriate voice: show enthusiasm for innovations, acknowledge research journey
5. Break mechanical patterns: vary paragraph lengths, allow some ideas to flow together
6. Remove meta-commentary and document status blocks

### Specific Before/After Edits

#### Edit 1: Eliminate "dramatically"
**Before (Line 130):**
> Computational efficiency improves dramatically.

**After:**
> Computational efficiency improves by orders of magnitude.

*Rationale:* "Dramatically" is an AI favorite. "Orders of magnitude" is technical and specific.

---

#### Edit 2: Replace "Remarkably"
**Before (Line 173):**
> Remarkably, the same U-Net architecture functions across geometry types without modification.

**After:**
> The same U-Net architecture---without any modification---functions across geometry types.

*Rationale:* Remove the AI tell-word; restructure for emphasis using em-dashes.

---

#### Edit 3: Diversify "However"
**Examples of replacement patterns:**

| Original | Replacement Options |
|----------|---------------------|
| However, when applied to... | When applied to..., though,... |
| However, the method is... | The method is..., but... |
| However, ultrasound elastography... | Ultrasound elastography, though,... |
| However, it requires... | But it requires... |
| However, boundary measurements... | Yet boundary measurements... |

*Rationale:* Reducing "However," frequency from 14 to 4-5 occurrences.

---

#### Edit 4: Remove "elegant"

**After:**
> The approach requires only commodity video equipment and avoids any physical contact with the sample.

*Rationale:* "Elegant" is an AI aesthetic judgment; replace with concrete description.
