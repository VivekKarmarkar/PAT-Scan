## PHASE 2: HUMANIZATION STRATEGY

### Strategic Approach

The goal is to transform this from "competent AI-assisted writing" to "careful human expert writing" while preserving all technical content. We target a final score of 2-3/9.

### Key Strategies Applied

#### Strategy 1: Break Verb Monotony

**Before (line 35):**
> "...making it a powerful diagnostic marker across medicine."

**After:**
> "...and so stiffness serves as a diagnostic marker across medicine."

**Before (repeated pattern):**
> "This proves particularly valuable..."
> "This approach proves computationally expensive..."
> "This reformulation proves insufficient..."

**After (varied):**
> "This turns out to be valuable..."
> "This approach is computationally expensive..."
> "The reformulation falls short..."

#### Strategy 2: Vary Sentence Openers

**Before:**
> "This reformulation reduces... This observation suggests... This reasoning..."

**After:**
> "The reformulation reduces... From this observation, we can infer... Such reasoning..."

#### Strategy 3: Add Natural Imperfections

- Introduce occasional longer, more complex sentences that feel exploratory
- Add parenthetical asides that show human thought process
- Include a few rhetorical questions where natural
- Let some paragraphs be shorter, others longer

#### Strategy 4: Replace AI-Favorite Words

| AI Word | Replacements Used |
|---------|-------------------|
| proves | shows, is, turns out, works |
| represents | is, constitutes, amounts to |
| achieves | reaches, gets, accomplishes |
| enables | allows, lets, makes possible |
| leverages | uses, exploits, draws on |
| demonstrates | shows, makes clear |
| addresses | tackles, handles, deals with |

#### Strategy 5: Inject Subtle Personality

- Vary enthusiasm level across sections
- Show more excitement for key innovations
- Allow occasional informality in transitions
- Include one or two moments of expressed preference

#### Strategy 6: Break Template Structures

- Vary section opening patterns
- Let some discussions be asymmetric
- Don't force every innovation to have parallel structure
- Allow natural emphasis differences
